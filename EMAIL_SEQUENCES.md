## Implementation Guide

### How to Use These Templates

1. **Copy the template** into your email marketing platform
2. **Customize the details** (names, company info, links, etc.)
3. **Set up the automation** based on the trigger (lead magnet signup, form submission, etc.)
4. **Test the emails** before going live
5. **Monitor performance** (open rates, click rates, conversions)

### Recommended Email Platform

- **Mailchimp** - Free for up to 500 contacts
- **ConvertKit** - Best for creators and bloggers
- **ActiveCampaign** - Advanced automation and CRM
- **HubSpot** - All-in-one marketing platform

### Key Metrics to Track

- **Open Rate** - Target: 25-35%
- **Click Rate** - Target: 5-10%
- **Conversion Rate** - Target: 2-5%
- **Unsubscribe Rate** - Keep below 0.5%

### Best Practices

1. **Personalize** - Use {{FIRST_NAME}}, {{COMPANY}}, etc.
2. **Keep it short** - Most people skim emails
3. **One CTA per email** - Don't overwhelm with options
4. **Test subject lines** - A/B test for better open rates
5. **Mobile-optimize** - 60%+ of emails are opened on mobile
6. **Include your contact info** - Phone, email, WhatsApp
7. **Unsubscribe link** - Required by law (CAN-SPAM)
